Stripe payouts now arrive in Xero
already coded

Every card payment the club takes is settled by Stripe as one lump deposit. Splitting that deposit back into what was actually sold has been done by hand, every day. It no longer has to be.

Live since 9 September 2026 · ANZ Business Premium 01-0635-0374823-00

The problem, in one line

A Stripe deposit lands in the bank with a reference like TRF HQKTQZ7G STRIPE. That is everything the bank tells us. It does not say which programme, how many families, or how much of it was Stripe's fee. Someone has to know, and then type it in.

Every payout from 15 April to 9 September was read back and re-split. All 55 reconciled to the cent. The $5.34 that could not be identified was thirteen one-dollar card tests.

The coding is not new, and it is not ours. It was read out of 25 payouts Olga and Natalia had already coded by hand, so the accounts match what they have been doing all year: FUNiño to 200/05, holiday camps to 203, Mini Football to 203/02, Technification to 200/07, facility hire to 301, the Stripe fee to 484/01.

On one payout they had already completed, the automatic split agreed to within 32 cents on $725.39. The difference is a Stripe account charge they netted against income and this treats as a fee.

What has not changed

  • Nothing is reconciled automatically. The entry is prepared and left unreconciled. Olga or Natalia still open the bank line and click OK, and either can delete it.
  • Nothing is invented. If a payment cannot be identified, or an account has not been agreed, the payout is not posted at all rather than filed somewhere plausible.
  • Xero still owns the ledger. No rule, report or existing transaction was altered.

Two things for you, Victor

Confirm the accounts

The mapping above reproduces current practice. It has not been reviewed by an accountant, and current practice is not the same as agreed practice. Two we would particularly like your view on:

Field hire income is coded to 301 Field Hire, which is an expense account tagged GST on Expenses. 275 Field Hire Income exists. Roughly $975 has gone through it since April.

Card-paid invoices post to 200/11 Event Revenue. Where an invoice already exists in Xero, the payment should clear that invoice instead. Say the word and those are handled separately.

Five categories are deliberately left blank until you set them: tournament entries, club events, memberships, United Prints and gymnastics. Any payout containing one of those will simply not post.
