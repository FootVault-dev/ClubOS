Before you fill this in

  • Nothing here changes Xero. Saving records the decision. ClubOS then posts one Receive Money per payout, with a line per category, and the accounts team clicks the match on the bank line. Xero does not let any software reconcile a bank line itself.
  • Every payout balances or none of it posts. Gross, less Stripe’s fees, plus any returned payout, must equal the payout to the cent. A split that half-matches is worse than one that does not match at all, because it looks finished.
  • A blank is better than a guess. A wrong account still balances perfectly, so nobody would ever notice it. ClubOS refuses to post a category nobody has confirmed.
  • Splitting later is cheap. If team entries and player shares should share one account now and separate ones later, that is a row in a table, not a change to the software.
